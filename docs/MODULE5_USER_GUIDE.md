# Module 5 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Inventory Management Guide

### Step 1: Multi-Warehouse Stock Registry
1. Navigate to `/inventory/stock`.
2. Inspect batch balances across bonded warehouses and cold storage facilities.

### Step 2: Stock Movements Execution
1. Navigate to `/inventory/movements`.
2. Execute inward receipts, export dispatches, or transfers with real-time stock balance validation.

### Step 3: Reorder Alerts & Low Stock Warnings
1. Navigate to `/inventory/reorder-alerts`.
2. Monitor low stock warnings and generate purchase order triggers.
