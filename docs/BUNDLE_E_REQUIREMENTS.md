# Bundle E Requirements Specification: Trade Finance, Banking, LC Checker, eBRC Reconciliation & Export Incentives

## 1. Executive Overview
Bundle E delivers the digitized Letter of Credit (LC) UCP 600 discrepancy auditor, central bank eBRC / EDPMS inward remittance reconciliation engine, multi-currency invoicing with FX gain/loss realization, and export incentive ledger (RoDTEP & Duty Drawback).

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **Letter of Credit (LC) UCP 600 Auditor**: Scans documentary credit rules against Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Bill of Lading datasets prior to bank presentation, detecting late presentation, expired validity, transshipment violations, or weight discrepancies under UCP 600 / ISBP 745.
2. **eBRC & EDPMS Reconciliation Engine**: Reconciles Inward Remittances (IRM) with Shipping Bills for eBRC generation and computes RoDTEP (1.4% FOB) & Duty Drawback (1.5% FOB) incentive claims.
3. **Multi-Currency Invoicing & FX Realization**: Issues foreign currency commercial export invoices, tracks booking vs realized spot exchange rates, and ledgers realized forex gains.
4. **Export Incentive Ledger**: Tracks DGFT RoDTEP e-scrip credits and Customs Duty Drawback claims.
