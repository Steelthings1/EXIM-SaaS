# Bundle E End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Trade Finance & Incentives Guide

### Step 1: Letter of Credit (LC) UCP 600 Auditor
1. Navigate to `/banking/lc-checker`.
2. Input LC terms (expiry, latest shipment date, transshipment rules) and actual shipping document figures.
3. Click **Audit Documents Against UCP 600** to detect discrepancy risks before negotiating bank presentation.

### Step 2: eBRC & EDPMS Remittance Reconciliation
1. Navigate to `/banking/ebrc-reconciliation`.
2. Match foreign exchange Inward Remittances (IRM) with ICEGATE Shipping Bills to close RBI EDPMS entries and generate electronic eBRCs.

### Step 3: Multi-Currency Invoicing & FX Realization
1. Navigate to `/finance/multi-currency-invoices`.
2. Issue foreign currency commercial export invoices and track booking vs realized spot exchange rate gains.

### Step 4: Export Incentive Realization Ledger
1. Navigate to `/incentives/ledger`.
2. Monitor DGFT RoDTEP e-scrip credits (1.4% FOB) and Duty Drawback (1.5% FOB) claims.
