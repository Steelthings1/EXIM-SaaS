# Bundle A End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Single-Entry Hub Guide

### Step 1: Manage Customer & Supplier CRM
1. Navigate to `/crm/contacts`.
2. Add international buyers and suppliers with KYB verification tax IDs, credit limits, and payment terms.

### Step 2: Register Products & Packaging Specs
1. Navigate to `/products/catalog`.
2. Add SKUs with HS tariff codes, net/gross weights, unit prices, and volume CBM factors.

### Step 3: Single-Entry Trade Order Workbench
1. Navigate to `/documents/single-entry-workbench`.
2. Select buyer, seller, ports, and line item quantities.
3. Observe live automatic recalculation of CIF totals, gross weights, CBM volumes, and carton package counts.
4. Toggle document preview tabs to inspect auto-populated Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, PO, and Shipping Instructions.
