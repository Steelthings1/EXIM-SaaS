# Module 5 Requirements Specification: Multi-Warehouse Inventory & Batch Tracking Engine

## 1. Executive Overview
Module 5 delivers the multi-warehouse inventory stock ledger, batch/lot number tracking, inward/outward stock movement execution, and reorder point warning triggers.

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **Stock Movement Engine**: Executes inward receipts, outward export dispatches, inter-warehouse transfers, and bin adjustments with insufficient stock guards.
2. **Reorder Point Warning Engine**: Evaluates stock levels against minimum thresholds and triggers low-stock alerts.
3. **Multi-Warehouse Batch Registry**: Real-time quantity balances across ICD bonded warehouses and cold storage facilities.
