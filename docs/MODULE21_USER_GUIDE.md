# Module 21 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Banking Auditor & EDPMS Guide

### Step 1: Letter of Credit UCP 600 Auditor V3
1. Navigate to `/banking/lc-auditor-v3`.
2. Input LC terms, invoice values, weights, and shipment dates. Click "Execute UCP 600 Pre-Presentation Discrepancy Audit" to identify non-compliance risks before bank submission.

### Step 2: Central Bank eBRC & EDPMS Closures
1. Navigate to `/banking/edpms-closures-v3`.
2. Match customs Shipping Bills with Inward Remittance References (IRM) to generate electronic Bank Realisation Certificates (eBRC) and mark EDPMS records CLOSED.
