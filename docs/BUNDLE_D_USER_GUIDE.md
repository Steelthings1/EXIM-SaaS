# Bundle D End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Logistics & Marine Insurance Guide

### Step 1: Multi-Carrier Freight Rate Aggregator
1. Navigate to `/logistics/freight-rates`.
2. Compare spot ocean rates across Maersk, MSC, and CMA CGM with itemized THC and BAF surcharges.

### Step 2: Carrier Bookings Registry
1. Navigate to `/logistics/bookings`.
2. Manage vessel allocations, voyage numbers, container numbers, and high-security bolt seal numbers.

### Step 3: Satellite AIS Vessel Live Telemetry
1. Navigate to `/logistics/ais-tracking`.
2. Track real-time satellite GPS vessel positions, speed in knots, and port gate-in events.

### Step 4: Marine Cargo Insurance Vault
1. Navigate to `/insurance/marine-policies`.
2. Issue Institute Cargo Clauses (A) All-Risks policies with 110% CIF sum insured valuation.
