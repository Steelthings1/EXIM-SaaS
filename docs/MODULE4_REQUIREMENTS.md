# Module 4 Requirements Specification: Customer & Supplier CRM with KYB Verification

## 1. Executive Overview
Module 4 delivers the international Customer and Supplier Relationship Management (CRM) master registry, Know Your Business (KYB) statutory identity verifier, credit limit management, and communication interaction logs.

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **KYB Statutory Identity Engine**: Validates tax registration numbers (GSTIN 15-char, UAE TRN 15-char, UK VAT 9-digit) and computes corporate credit risk scores.
2. **CRM Master Registry**: Multi-currency credit limit management, payment terms, and KYB audit status badges.
3. **Interaction Communication Logs**: Log workspace for trade inquiries, quotation requests, pricing negotiations, and payment reminders.
