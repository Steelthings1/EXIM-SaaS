# Module 4 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step CRM & KYB Guide

### Step 1: Customer & Supplier CRM Master
1. Navigate to `/crm/contacts`.
2. Manage international buyers and suppliers, multi-currency credit limits, and payment terms.

### Step 2: KYB Statutory Identity Verifier
1. Navigate to `/crm/kyb-verifier`.
2. Input buyer tax registration numbers (GSTIN, UAE TRN, UK VAT) to run automated statutory format audits and credit risk scoring.

### Step 3: Interaction Communication Logs
1. Navigate to `/crm/interactions`.
2. Record trade inquiries, quotation requests, pricing negotiations, and payment reminders.
