# Bundle D Requirements Specification: Logistics, Freight Aggregation, AIS Vessel Tracking & Marine Insurance

## 1. Executive Overview
Bundle D delivers the ocean/air freight rate aggregator, carrier booking confirmations, satellite AIS vessel tracking telemetry, and marine cargo insurance policy management.

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **Multi-Carrier Freight Aggregator**: Compares ocean and air freight quotes across carriers (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM) with surcharges breakdown (Terminal Handling Charges, Bunker Adjustment Factor, ISPS) and sorts by cost efficiency.
2. **Satellite AIS Vessel Tracking Engine**: Generates satellite AIS vessel milestone telemetry and tracks vessel berthing events.
3. **Carrier Bookings Registry**: Carrier booking confirmations, voyage numbers, container numbers, seal registrations, and ETD/ETA schedules.
4. **Marine Cargo Insurance Vault**: Institute Cargo Clauses (A) All-Risks policies, statutory 110% CIF sum insured calculations, and premium receipts.
