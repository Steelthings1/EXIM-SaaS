# Bundle B End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Commercial Workflow Guide

### Step 1: Visual 26-Step Trade Pipeline Tracker
1. Navigate to `/workflow/pipeline-tracker`.
2. Monitor trade order progress across all 21 core milestones (from Lead to Shipping Bill, eBRC foreign exchange realization, and GST drawback).
3. Click **Advance 1-Click to Next Stage** to move the order forward in the state machine.

### Step 2: Proforma Quotations
1. Navigate to `/sales/quotations`.
2. Generate formal price offers with Incoterms 2020 freight estimates and validity dates.

### Step 3: International Sales Contracts & AI Risk Auditor
1. Navigate to `/sales/contracts`.
2. Review executed agreements under UN CISG 1980 governing law and SIAC/LCIA arbitration forums.
3. Click **Run AI CISG Legal Audit** to analyze legal risk scores and Incoterms liability shifts.

### Step 4: Vendor Procurement POs
1. Navigate to `/procurement/orders`.
2. Issue vendor POs for raw materials and packaging supplies.
