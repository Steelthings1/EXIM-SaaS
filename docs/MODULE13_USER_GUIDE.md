# Module 13 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Marine Cargo Insurance & Claims Guide

### Step 1: Marine Cargo Insurance Vault
1. Navigate to `/insurance/policies`.
2. Issue policies by entering order CIF value, selecting Institute Cargo Clause (A/B/C), and viewing automatic 110% CIF sum insured valuation.

### Step 2: Transit Damage Claims Ledger
1. Navigate to `/insurance/claims`.
2. Lodge cargo damage claims, upload surveyor loss reports, and track claim settlement progress.
