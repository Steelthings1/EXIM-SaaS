# Module 24 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Notifications & In-App Alerts Guide

### Step 1: In-App Notification Center
1. Click the Notification Bell in top header navigation or visit `/notifications/center`.
2. View real-time alert feed, filter by category tags, and click "Mark Read" to clear unread badge counts.

### Step 2: Delivery Channel Preferences
1. Navigate to `/notifications/preferences`.
2. Toggle delivery channels (In-App Bell, Email Digest, SMS Webhooks) for operational alerts.
