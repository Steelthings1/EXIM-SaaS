# Bundle B Requirements Specification: Commercial Workflow, Quotations, Contracts & 26-Step Pipeline Engine

## 1. Executive Overview
Bundle B delivers the commercial workflow engine, export quotations & proforma invoices, UN CISG 1980 compliant international sales contracts with AI risk auditing, vendor purchase orders, and the visual 26-step trade pipeline tracker.

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **Visual 26-Step Trade Pipeline Tracker**: State machine managing trade order lifecycle across 21 core sequential milestones (Lead $\rightarrow$ Proforma Invoice $\rightarrow$ Contract $\rightarrow$ Production $\rightarrow$ Packaging $\rightarrow$ Shipping Bill $\rightarrow$ Dispatch $\rightarrow$ Payment Realization/eBRC $\rightarrow$ Archive) with 1-click stage advancement.
2. **AI Contract Review Agent**: Audits international sales agreements against UN CISG 1980 rules, Incoterms 2020 liability shifts, arbitration forums (SIAC, LCIA, MCIA), and payment term credit risk.
3. **Export Proforma Quotations**: Commercial price offers, freight estimates, and validity windows.
4. **Vendor Procurement POs**: Raw material and packaging purchase order tracking.
