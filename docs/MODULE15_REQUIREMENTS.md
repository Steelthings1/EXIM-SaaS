# Module 15 Requirements Specification: Multi-Modal AI Copilot Workspace

## 1. Executive Overview
Module 15 delivers multi-modal AI Copilot capabilities, including Document RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Chat, Voice Assistant Command processing, and Computer Vision Packaging Label Inspection for export food regulations (FDA, EU, GCC).

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **Multi-Modal AI Copilot Engine**: Processes Document RAG queries across trade contracts, LCs, and invoices, and executes voice assistant commands.
2. **Packaging Label Vision Scanner**: Audits product packaging labels against destination market regulations (FDA USA, EU EFSA, GCC GSO dual Arabic text, FSSAI).
