# Module 10 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step International Sales Contracts & Legal Audit Guide

### Step 1: International Sales Contracts (UN CISG 1980)
1. Navigate to `/sales/contracts-v2`.
2. Build sales agreements with buyer/seller entities, Incoterms, UN CISG governing law, and SIAC/LCIA arbitration forum designations.

### Step 2: AI Contract Legal Risk Auditor Scanner
1. Navigate to `/sales/contract-audit`.
2. Input Incoterm points, contract values, and payment credit terms to execute legal risk audits.
