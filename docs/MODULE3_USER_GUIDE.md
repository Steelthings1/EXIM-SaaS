# Module 3 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Product Catalog & Document Engine Guide

### Step 1: Product Catalog Management
1. Navigate to `/products/catalog`.
2. Manage export SKUs, HS codes, unit weights, and CBM container volumes.

### Step 2: Bill of Materials (BOM) & Origin Calculation
1. Navigate to `/products/bom`.
2. Input raw material component costs and origin to verify domestic value addition % for preferential Certificate of Origin (CoO).

### Step 3: Single-Entry Order Workbench
1. Navigate to `/documents/single-entry-workbench`.
2. Input order figures once to auto-calculate CIF totals, weights, and CBM volumes.
3. Click **1-Click Generate Document Suite** to produce Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Shipping Bill, and Certificate of Origin.
