# Module 2 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Compliance Engine Guide

### Step 1: AI HS Code Classification
1. Navigate to `/compliance/hs-classifier`.
2. Enter your product description (e.g., "Organic roasted Arabica coffee beans").
3. Select your target customs jurisdiction (e.g. India, USA, UAE, EU).
4. View predicted national HS code, AI confidence rating, and WCO tariff hierarchy tree.

### Step 2: Destination Market Regulatory Checklist
1. Navigate to `/compliance/country-rules`.
2. Select destination country and enter HS tariff code.
3. Review mandatory pre-shipment certificates (Phytosanitary, CoA, Halal), food-grade packaging standards, and dual-language labeling rules.

### Step 3: Calculate Landed Cost & Free Trade Agreement (FTA) Savings
1. Navigate to `/compliance/tariff-calculator`.
2. Enter shipment CIF value, HS code, exporter, and importer countries.
3. Toggle **Apply Preferential FTA Tariff (CEPA / ECTA)** to view duty savings.

### Step 4: Fuzzy Sanctions Screening
1. Navigate to `/compliance/sanctions-screening`.
2. Enter buyer, seller, vessel, or freight forwarder name.
3. Adjust similarity threshold slider to screen against OFAC, UN, EU, and UK denied party lists.
