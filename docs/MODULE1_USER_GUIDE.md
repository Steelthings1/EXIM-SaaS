# Module 1 End-User Setup Guide

## Quick Start Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Configure Organization & Legal Profile
1. Navigate to `/settings/organization` in the EXIM.IM dashboard.
2. Enter your Registered Legal Name, Trade Name, and Legal Entity Classification.
3. Select your primary trade invoicing currency (e.g. USD, EUR, INR).
4. Save settings.

### Step 2: Upload Statutory Credentials to Identity Vault
1. Navigate to `/settings/statutory-vault`.
2. Drag and drop your statutory certificates (GSTIN registration, IEC certificate, EORI PDF).
3. The AI License Vision Parser will extract details and validate format compliance automatically.
4. Verify the status badge switches to **Statutory Verified**.

### Step 3: Register Operating Branches & ICD Warehouses
1. Navigate to `/settings/branches-warehouses`.
2. Register your head office, regional branches, seaport codes (e.g., INNSA1), and Inland Container Depots (ICD).
3. Assign bonded customs storage capacities.

### Step 4: Invite Team Members & Assign RBAC Roles
1. Navigate to `/settings/members-roles`.
2. Click **Invite Team Member / External Partner**.
3. Select from 19 RBAC roles (e.g., Export Manager, Customs Broker CHA, Auditor).
4. Send invitation.
