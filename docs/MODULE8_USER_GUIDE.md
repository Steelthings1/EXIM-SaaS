# Module 8 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Procurement & GRN Guide

### Step 1: Vendor Purchase Orders
1. Navigate to `/procurement/purchase-orders`.
2. Issue purchase orders to raw material suppliers, specify line items, quantities, unit prices, and expected delivery dates.

### Step 2: Goods Receipt Notes (GRN)
1. Navigate to `/procurement/goods-receipt`.
2. Record inward stock receipts, log accepted vs rejected quantities, and verify quality acceptance rates.
