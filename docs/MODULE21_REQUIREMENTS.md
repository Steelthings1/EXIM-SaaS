# Module 21 Requirements Specification: Banking, LC UCP 600 Auditor & EDPMS Reconciliation

## 1. Executive Overview
Module 21 delivers Letter of Credit (LC) UCP 600 & ISBP 745 discrepancy auditing, central bank eBRC / RBI EDPMS inward remittance reconciliation, and Authorised Dealer (AD) Category-I bank presentation ledgers.

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **Banking Auditor Engine**: Audits LC clauses against Commercial Invoices, Packing Lists, and Bills of Lading prior to bank presentation (detecting late presentation, value overruns, transshipment prohibitions, or gross weight mismatches) and reconciles IRM remittances for central bank eBRC generation.
2. **LC UCP 600 Auditor Workspace**: Pre-presentation document audit workspace for evaluating LC compliance under UCP 600 rules.
3. **eBRC & EDPMS Reconciliation Workspace**: Central bank eBRC and IRM remittance matching dashboard.
