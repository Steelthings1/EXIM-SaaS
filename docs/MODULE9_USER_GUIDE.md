# Module 9 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Export Sales & Quotations Guide

### Step 1: Export Sales Quotations
1. Navigate to `/sales/export-quotations`.
2. Build price offers with buyer details, Incoterms, subtotal, freight/insurance additions, and validity windows.

### Step 2: Profit Margin Analysis
1. Navigate to `/sales/margin-analysis`.
2. Enter cost and revenue components to calculate gross profit and gross margin percentages.
