# Module 3 Requirements Specification: Product Catalog, BOM & 1-Click Document Suite

## 1. Executive Overview
Module 3 delivers the Product Master SKU catalog, Bill of Materials (BOM) raw material breakdown, and automated 1-click document suite generation (Commercial Invoices, Packing Lists, Shipping Bills, Certificates of Origin, Proforma Invoices, Purchase Orders, and Shipping Instructions).

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **Product Master Catalog**: Export SKU master registry detailing HS Codes, net/gross weights, and CBM volume factors.
2. **Bill of Materials (BOM)**: Raw material component breakdown and domestic value-addition tracking for origin determination.
3. **Single-Entry Engine**: Auto-calculates subtotal, total CIF amounts, gross/net weights, container CBM volume factors, and carton package counts from a single master trade order.
4. **1-Click Document Suite Generator**: Automatically populates and generates 7 official trade documents from a single master order input.
