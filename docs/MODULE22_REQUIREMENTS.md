# Module 22 Requirements Specification: Export Incentive Realization Ledger

## 1. Executive Overview
Module 22 delivers the export incentive claim calculator (RoDTEP, RoSCTL, Duty Drawback Section 74/75, Advance Authorization), DGFT/Customs e-scrip scroll generator, and credit balance trading ledger.

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **Export Incentive Engine**: Calculates RoDTEP, RoSCTL, and Duty Drawback claim amounts as a percentage of FOB realization value INR, generates DGFT e-scrip scroll records, and tracks available credit balances.
2. **Export Incentive Claims Workspace**: Claim tracking workspace displaying RoDTEP and Duty Drawback scroll numbers, FOB values INR, and realization statuses.
3. **DGFT e-Scrip Credit Balance Workspace**: Credit ledger workspace for monitoring active e-scrip scroll balances and duty offset utilization against import declarations.
