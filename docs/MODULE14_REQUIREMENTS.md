# Module 14 Requirements Specification: Trade Finance, Banking & LC UCP 600 Auditor

## 1. Executive Overview
Module 14 delivers the digitized Letter of Credit (LC) UCP 600 & ISBP 745 discrepancy auditor, central bank eBRC / EDPMS inward remittance reconciliation engine, and export incentive realization ledger (RoDTEP & Duty Drawback).

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **LC UCP 600 Auditor**: Audits LC clauses against Commercial Invoices, Packing Lists, and Bills of Lading prior to bank submission (detecting late presentation, value overruns, transshipment prohibitions, or weight mismatches).
2. **eBRC & EDPMS Reconciliation Engine**: Matches Inward Remittance References (IRM) against Customs Shipping Bills to close RBI EDPMS records.
3. **Export Incentive Ledger**: Calculates 1.4% RoDTEP e-scrips and 1.5% Duty Drawback refunds based on FOB shipping bill values.
