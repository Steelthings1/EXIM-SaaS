# Module 14 End-User Setup & Usage Guide

## Step-by-Step Trade Finance & Banking Guide

### Step 1: Letter of Credit UCP 600 Auditor
1. Navigate to `/banking/lc-auditor`.
2. Input LC terms, invoice values, shipping dates, and weights to execute UCP 600 pre-presentation discrepancy audits.

### Step 2: eBRC & EDPMS Reconciliation
1. Navigate to `/banking/edpms-reconciliation`.
2. Match Inward Remittance Reference (IRM) numbers with Shipping Bills to generate eBRC certificates and close EDPMS files.

### Step 3: Export Incentive Ledger
1. Navigate to `/incentives/export-ledger`.
2. Input FOB export values to compute RoDTEP e-scrip scroll balances and Duty Drawback claims.
