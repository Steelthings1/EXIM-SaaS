# Module 10 Requirements Specification: International Sales Contracts & AI Legal Auditor

## 1. Executive Overview
Module 10 delivers UN CISG 1980-compliant international sale of goods agreements, Incoterms 2020 risk allocation, arbitration forum designations (SIAC, LCIA, MCIA), and AI contract legal risk auditing.

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **International Sales Contracts Engine**: Manages contract agreement drafting, governing law designations, and status transitions (`DRAFT` → `PENDING_SIGNATURE` → `EXECUTED` → `TERMINATED`).
2. **AI Contract Legal Risk Auditor**: Evaluates Incoterm risk shifts (e.g. EXW vs FCA vs DDP destination tax liabilities) and open account credit penalties.
