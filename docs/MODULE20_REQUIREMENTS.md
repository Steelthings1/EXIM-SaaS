# Module 20 Requirements Specification: Multi-Currency Finance, Forex Treasury & Invoicing

## 1. Executive Overview
Module 20 delivers multi-currency commercial invoicing, zero-rated GST Letter of Undertaking (LUT) tagging, foreign exchange treasury management, and spot vs bank remittance exchange rate gain/loss realization calculation.

## 2. Technical Capabilities
1. **Forex Treasury Engine**: Calculates base currency INR equivalents, evaluates spot vs bank remittance realization exchange rates, and computes realized exchange gains or losses.
2. **Multi-Currency Invoicing Workspace**: Workspace for issuing commercial export invoices (USD, EUR, GBP, AED) tagged under zero-rated GST LUT rules.
